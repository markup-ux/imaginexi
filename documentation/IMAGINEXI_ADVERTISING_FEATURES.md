## One-paragraph pitch

ImagineXI is a **level-75–focused** private server built on a modern open-source Final Fantasy XI core, with **custom combat rules for notorious monsters**, **job and gear design tuned to how this server plays**, and **quality-of-life** that respects a classic pace. Progression, traits, and unlocks are kept **usable at cap** so endgame builds stay coherent.

## Feature highlights (what players feel in-game)

### Progression and jobs

- **75-cap coherence:** Traits, skills, and spells are aligned so **jobs play through to cap** without “dead” unlocks sitting past the server’s intended ceiling.
- **Early job identity:** Several jobs gain **meaningful traits at low level** (examples called out in design: dual wield on more jobs, treasure hunter, gilfinder, auto refresh, clear mind, conserve MP, fast cast) so **subjobs and leveling** feel less sparse.
- **Scholar:** **Stratagem charges** and timing are customized; **Accession** and **Manifestation** work as **time windows** that can apply to **multiple eligible spells** (similar in feel to blue-mage diffusion-style play), not one-shot consumption per spell.
- **Summoner:** **Avatar perpetuation MP drain is removed** at the engine level; blood-pact delay gear is **remapped** to stats that still matter; **item lines** were reviewed so pet-related bonuses stay **sensible for the job** that equips them.
- **Beastmaster:** Charm-focused mechanics are adjusted so **main-job charm** fits the server’s rules (including how certain gear bonuses are interpreted).
- **Dual pets (SMN / BST / DRG):** With **dual-pet mode** on, eligible jobs get a **one-time short system message** (e.g. pet slot **1/2**) after the first summon or charm, hinting that a **second slot** may be usable when rules allow.




### Notorious monsters and combat

- **HNMs sleep-immune** to sleep and lullaby effects—fights stay **action-based**, not lockdown loops.

### World, economy, and QoL

- **Conquest point exchange** beside signet and regional overseers: trade **conquest points, gil, and other tracked currencies** through a purpose-built menu (weights tuned to a **single ladder** so math stays consistent).
- **Mounts:** **All mounts unlocked early** (low level) for travel flavor without gating behind late progression.


- **Fame:** **Killing beastmen** can adjust fame in line with custom reputation design.
- **Selected instances:** Some **dungeon entry requirements relaxed** for smoother party access.

### Trust, support, and misc systems

- **Trust companions** tuned (spell lists and related data) for balance under custom perpetuation and timer rules.
- **Pixie-type enemies** may cast **support magic** in a rescue-oriented pattern (custom spell list behavior).
